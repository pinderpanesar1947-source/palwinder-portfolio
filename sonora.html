Project Overview

SONORA is a conceptual premium music streaming experience designed to break away from the cluttered interfaces of current industry leaders. The goal was to focus purely on immersive discovery, high-fidelity audio visualization, and spatial interactions that feel physically rewarding to touch.

UX Process & Concept

The research phase involved interviewing audiophiles and casual listeners. A recurring pain point was "decision fatigue" caused by overwhelmed algorithmic feeds. SONORA introduces a spatial, gesture-based exploration map, turning passive scrolling into an active curation process.
